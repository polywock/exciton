## Exciton Voice commands for Blender

Press and hold Q and say your command:

- Views: "Top", "Front", "Camera", "Frame Selected"
- Adding objects: "Cube", "Monkey", "UV Sphere", "Point Light"
- Things buried in menus: "Recalculate Normals", "Merge at Center", "Shade Smooth", "Origin to Geometry"

Handy for:

- Learning Blender, without memorizing hundreds of shortcuts
- Sculpting or painting with a tablet, stylus in hand
- Working without a numpad
- Staying in the flow, instead of digging through menus

## Install

Follow [exciton.dev/download](https://exciton.dev/download). Blender installs Exciton from its extension repository and
keeps it up to date. Then, in the 3D Viewport, press <kbd>N</kbd>, open the Voice Control tab and sign in.

Each version's zip is also on [Releases](https://github.com/polywock/exciton/releases), to install from disk. Blender
can't update an add-on installed that way.

## Requirements

- Blender 5.0 or later on Windows, or 5.1 or later on Mac. Linux hasn't been tested yet.
- A microphone and an internet connection: speech is recognized online.
- A free account at [exciton.dev](https://exciton.dev).

Learn more: [exciton.dev](https://exciton.dev)
